How to Use the
TEL CRM Dashboard

This guide walks you through every part of the CRM — from logging in for the first time to managing leads, sending WhatsApp follow-ups, and reading your performance data.

🌐 gnolegroup.github.io/github-crm-dashboard
🔑

Getting Started

1
Open the CRM in your browser
Go to gnolegroup.github.io/github-crm-dashboard — bookmark it so you can find it quickly every day. Works on Chrome, Edge, and phone browsers.
2
Enter your email and password
Your login details will be given to you by your Admin. Type your email address and password, then click Sign In — or press Enter after typing your password.
3
Forgot your password?
Click the Forgot password? link below the Sign In button. Type your email when prompted — you will receive a reset link in your inbox within a few minutes.
4
You are now inside the CRM
You will see the Home Dashboard with your leads summary. The navigation bar at the top lets you switch between all the sections of the CRM.
✅
Stay logged in. The CRM automatically logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity to keep your data secure. Just log back in when that happens.

Adding a New Lead

1
Click + Add Lead
Find the green button at the top of the Leads page.
2
Fill in the lead's details
Name — customer's full name
Phone — local number only, e.g. 8123456 (no +673)
Assigned Agent — select yourself or the agent handling this lead
Gender — male or female
Age Group — select the range (e.g. 25–34)
District & Area — select district first, then the area will appear
Source — where the lead came from (Roadshow, Facebook, TikTok, etc.)
Product — what product they are interested in
Priority — hot / warm / cold
Stage — usually New when first adding
Date — when this lead was generated
Date of Birth — optional, used for birthday reminders
Notes / Remarks — anything important to remember
3
Click Save Lead
The lead is saved immediately to the database. It will appear in your leads list with a lead number (L-0001, L-0002, etc.).

Editing a Lead

1
Find the lead
Use the Search box to search by name or phone, or use the filter dropdowns to narrow by stage, priority, or product.
2
Click the pencil (✏️) icon
Each lead row has an edit button on the right side. Click it to open the edit panel.
3
Update the details and save
Change the stage, add a remark, update priority — anything you need. Every edit is recorded in the lead's activity log.

Sending a WhatsApp Message from a Lead

Each lead has a WhatsApp button (💬). Clicking it opens WhatsApp Web with the customer's number and a pre-filled message already written. You just need to press Send in WhatsApp — the CRM never sends anything automatically.

ℹ️
Lead numbering: Leads are numbered from oldest (L-0001) to newest (highest number). The newest lead always appears at the top of the list. Your lead numbers never change.

Follow-Ups

The Follow-Ups page shows you which leads need to be followed up today. The CRM automatically schedules 5 follow-up reminders for every new lead:

StepWhenTask
Step 1Day 1First contact — call or WhatsApp
Step 2Day 3Follow up — no response yet?
Step 3Day 7Send product info & quotation
Step 4Day 14Final follow up — close or mark lost
Step 5Day 30Re-engage — 1 month check-in

These dates are counted from the day the lead was created. You will see each step appear automatically on the Due tab when its day arrives.

TabWhat it shows
DueFollow-ups that fall on today's date (including overdue ones)
UpcomingScheduled for the next few days — so you can plan ahead
DoneFollow-ups you have already completed

How to complete a follow-up

1
Open the Follow-Ups page from the top navigation
2
Find the lead that is due
Leads are sorted so the most urgent appear first.
3
Click Send WhatsApp
This opens WhatsApp Web with a pre-written follow-up message. Send it to the customer.
4
Mark it as Done or Snooze
Done — follow-up completed, move to the next one.
Snooze — not ready yet, remind me again later.
⚠️
Do not ignore overdue follow-ups. Hot leads that are not followed up quickly may lose interest. Check this page every morning when you start work.

Birthday Reminders

The Birthdays page shows you customers whose birthdays are coming up in the next 7 days. Sending a birthday greeting is a great way to stay connected and build loyalty.

1
Go to Tools → Birthdays from the top navigation
2
Check the list of upcoming birthdays
Each card shows the customer's name, birthday, and a pre-written birthday message.
3
Edit the message if needed, then click Send WhatsApp
The message opens in WhatsApp Web, ready to send. You can personalise it before sending.
4
The birthday card is marked as sent
It will disappear from the pending list so you don't send it twice.
✅
For birthday reminders to work, the customer's Date of Birth must be saved in their lead profile. Make sure to fill it in when adding new leads.
💬

WhatsApp Send Queue

Found under Tools → WhatsApp Send. This page collects all pending birthday messages in one place, sorted by how soon the birthday is. It works the same as the Birthdays page but is designed for batch sending — useful when you have many birthdays in a week.

LabelMeaning
Today 🎂Birthday is today — send immediately
TomorrowBirthday is tomorrow
In X daysBirthday is coming up in a few days

Priority colours (red = very important, orange = important, grey = standard) help you know which customers to reach first.

Calendar & Special Days

Found in the top navigation under Calendar. Two sub-sections:

Calendar

A monthly calendar view showing Brunei public holidays. You can also load in the holidays for 2026 or 2027 from Settings if they are not showing.

Special Days

Shows upcoming national and Islamic holidays — Hari Raya, Sultan's Birthday, National Day, and more. You can send a bulk WhatsApp greeting to all your customers for each occasion.

1
Go to Calendar → Special Days
2
Select a holiday and click Send to all
The CRM will open WhatsApp for each customer one by one with a pre-filled greeting message.
3
Press Send in WhatsApp for each one
After sending, click Next in the CRM to move to the following customer. Repeat until all are done.
📱

Survey

The Survey section in the top navigation is used to record customer satisfaction or feedback responses. When a customer gives you feedback after a sale or follow-up, you can log it here so it is attached to their lead record.

Fill in the customer's name, select their lead, choose the rating or response, and save. The survey response will appear in the lead's activity history.

Import & Export Admin Manager Senior Agent

Exporting leads

On the Leads page, click the Export button (top right). Choose:

FormatUse it when
Excel (.xlsx)You want to open and edit the data in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
PDFYou want to print or share a formatted report

The export respects your current filters — if you have filtered by stage or product, only those leads will be exported.

Importing leads from Excel

1
Download the import template
Click Import in the top navigation, then click Download Template. This gives you an Excel file with the correct column headings.
2
Fill in the template
Keep the column headers exactly as they are. Fill in one row per lead.
Phone: local digits only (e.g. 8123456)
Gender: male or female (lowercase)
Date: format as YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2026-09-17)
Stage: New / Contacted / Pending / Closed / Lost
3
Upload the file
On the Import page, click Choose File, select your filled Excel file, then click Import. The CRM will tell you how many leads were added.
⚠️
Check before importing. Duplicate phone numbers may cause errors. Review your file first and remove any rows that already exist in the CRM.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Failed to fetch" on login The database has gone to sleep (this can happen after several days of no activity). Contact your Admin — they can wake it up in about 1 minute.
Page looks broken or outdated Press Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) to hard-refresh and load the latest version.
Cannot see my leads Check that you are logged in with the correct email. If your leads were assigned by name and not by your account, ask Admin to reassign them.
Forgot password Click Forgot password? on the login page and enter your email. A reset link will be sent to your inbox.
WhatsApp not opening Make sure WhatsApp Web is set up in your browser at web.whatsapp.com and you are logged in.
Lead not saving Check your internet connection. If offline, wait to reconnect and try again.
Something else is wrong Take a screenshot and send it to your Admin or IT support.
✅
Best practice: Log in every day even if briefly. Regular use keeps the database active and prevents it from going to sleep.
